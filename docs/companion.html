Decentralized CBO: the linear model

Interactive companion to the paper arXiv:2611.27433.

In the linear model, decentralized CBO is run without noise and each node is reduced to its consensus point. The iteration is

P′ = (1−a)P + aZ,    Z′ = −aP + WZ + aZ,    a = λ dt,

where P holds the positions of the N nodes, Z the tracking variables and W the mixing matrix. Before reaching consensus, the nodes and their tracking variables circle around each other, which the paper calls the consensus ballet.

On a ring the dynamics split into independent harmonics k, with eigenvalues γk = θ + (1−θ)cos(2πk/N).

Mode shapes: eigenvectors of L

With W = I − dt L, the modes are the eigenvectors of L. On a ring these are the Fourier modes cos(2πkj/N), sin(2πkj/N).

Modal amplitudes

μi(t) = ⟨P(t), vi⟩ are the coordinates of P in the eigenbasis, so P(t) = ∑i μi(t) vi. Each mode evolves independently of the others.
